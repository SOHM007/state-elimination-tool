How to Use

  1. Enter the DFA states, alphabet, start state, and final states.
  2. Click Build Transition Table.
  3. Enter the destination state for every state-symbol pair.
  4. Click Convert to Regular Expression.
  5. Read or download the result.

Notation: ε means an empty string, ∅ means no path, and * means zero or more repetitions.
